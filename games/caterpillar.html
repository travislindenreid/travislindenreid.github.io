caterpillar

2018

eat a boy... become a boy.

caterpillar is a small project I made about my personal experience with gender dysphoria. In it, you're a caterpillar crawling around on a bed of magazine scraps of men's body parts- which I cut out from actual magazines- and eating them to create a dream bod.

caterpillar was featured in the 2019 Indie Bits Festival, an event which highlights interactive media made by southern game developers.
