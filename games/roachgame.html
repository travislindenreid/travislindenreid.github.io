Roach Game

current project

Kill the roaches. Kill your landlord.

Roach Game is a work-in-progress horror-comedy game in which the player is squashing (literally) a roach uprising in their apartment while battling a mold poisoning induced psychosis and an inept apartment manager.

The roaches and environments in the game are photogrammetry scans of my actual apartment and roaches (and other bugs…) it contains.
